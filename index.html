Rendering Visualizer

Bidirectional path tracing, visualized: a CPU renderer and three.js visualizations of its light trajectories.

Each page below shows one thing, with every parameter fixed as a constant at the top of its main.ts. Where a page exists to compare kinds of thing — three integrators, three ways a path can end — that comparison is its one control; everything incidental stays a constant.

Act 0 — the renderer and the scenes

  • render-still — one scene by all five integrators (PT / NEE / BDPT / MMLT / ReSTIR), equal samples, with per-pass cost
  • scene-sheet — every scene in the registry at equal samples
  • material-strip — every material the renderer has, one sphere each
  • depth-sweep — the image at 1, 2, 3, 4 and 32 bounces

Act 1 — the vocabulary of one path

  • path-strategies — which algorithm builds the path: PT / PT+NEE / BDPT (switch)
  • path-outcomes — how a walk ends: emitter / roulette death / escape (switch)
  • connection-outcomes — one light connection, clear or blocked (switch)

Act 2 — populations of paths

  • path-swarm — 100 of one pixel's paths at once, churning; 1 / 2 / full length (switch)
  • light-swarm — 100 paths thrown from the lamp, connecting to the camera (switch)

ReSTIR (V4)

  • viz-restir-flow — reservoirs sharing light samples: source map, reuse lineage of any pixel, discoveries propagating

Metropolis (V3)

  • viz-mlt-chain — one Markov chain walking path space: proposals ghost in, accepts solidify, rejects evaporate, splats accumulate
