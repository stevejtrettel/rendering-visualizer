path-swarm

SHOWS: not one path but the population — a hundred of this pixel's samples on screen at once, ten of them retired and replaced every refresh, so the estimator is visible as a living spray rather than a single trajectory. At one bounce the spray is the material's scattering distribution; at full length it is the path family whose average is the pixel's colour, and you can see how few of its members ever touch the light.
