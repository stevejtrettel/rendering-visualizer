light-swarm

SHOWS: the transport run backwards. A hundred paths thrown out of the lamp (cyan), churning; from each of their vertices the renderer connects to the pinhole — green where that connection is clear, red where a sphere or wall blocks it, nothing at all when the point falls outside the frame. The connection is deterministic, not sampled, so most of them succeed. What makes light tracing different is where the energy goes: each success deposits into whichever pixel it happens to land in, rather than the pixel we set out to render — which is exactly why the film needs a separate splat channel for it.
