light-path-beauty

SHOWS: one complete transport path as a physical object. A single long walk thrown out of the lamp — eight bounces wandering the box (cyan) — whose final vertex sees the pinhole, closed by the green connection to a little camera. The switch chooses what lights the room: lit by the path dims the lamp to a whisper and lets the glowing walk throw its pools of colored light on the walls (stylized, converges slowly); lit by the lamp runs the ceiling emitter at its true radiance through a sampled area light, with the path as plain colored geometry (fully sampled, converges fast). Both are rendered with three-gpu-pathtracer, converging live. Orbit freely; the accumulation restarts on every move.
